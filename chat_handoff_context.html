3. The 2-Phase Analytical Lens

  • Phase A: Comprehensive News Context (~400 words) — Rigorous journalistic background, corporate players, timelines, and market dynamics.
  • Phase B: The 5-Dimensional Strategic Lens
    1. The Delta (What Changed): Metrics, baselines, and deal multiples.
    2. The Mechanism (How It Changed): Pricing, regulatory, or supply-chain driver.
    3. Strategic Impact: Margins, distributor ROIC, and channel conflict.
    4. Core Lesson: Transferable principle or mental model.
    5. Lateral Transfer: 2 cross-industry applications.
